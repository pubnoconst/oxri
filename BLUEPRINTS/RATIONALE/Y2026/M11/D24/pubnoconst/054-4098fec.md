## Context

The owner asked to "make the linen more natural by randomizing it; if we are using repeated texture, the texture size has to be bigger."

Two things made the old linen look artificial:
- **The grid:** each cloth was a single 280 × 280 px SVG noise tile, repeated, so the eye could find the 280 px grid.
- **Identical cards:** every card started the same tile at its own top-left corner, so a page of journal cards showed the identical patch of cloth on each one.

## Decision

- **Two layers per cloth** (the "cicada principle"):
  - The tiles are 307 px and 419 px. Both are prime and so coprime, and together they only line up again every 307 × 419 ≈ 129,000 px.
  - Each layer has distinct seeds (the Far layer's are shifted by 100). Each carries 0.75 of the old contrast, because two independent layers at 0.75 give about the contrast of one at full strength (0.75·√2 ≈ 1.06).
  - Grain (base frequencies) and the dark and light amplitudes are unchanged from the single-tile linen.
- **Cost:** the noise to rasterise per surface is 307² + 419² px, about 3.4× the old 280² tile, once per surface at first paint. One 1024 px tile would give similar freedom from repetition at about 13×. Desktop Chrome timing couldn't separate the two (about 15 ms either way at 2× scale), so the owner's phone is the real check.
- **A seeded weave per card.**
  - `linen.Offset(key)` hashes the key with FNV-1a into an inline `background-position` for the two linen layers. Journal cards use `entry:<id>`.
  - The same entry always gets the same cloth, and different entries get different pieces.
  - Inline style attributes are allowed by the CSP's `style-src 'unsafe-inline'`.
  - Single surfaces (page, login, settings, editors) keep their origin, since there's nothing beside them to match.
- **Generated in Go, single source.**
  - `views/linen` builds the four cloths (`--linen-page-light`, `--linen-card-light`, `--linen-page-dark`, `--linen-card-dark`) at start-up.
  - They're served as a content-hashed component stylesheet (`/views/linen/linen.css`), linked before `site.css`, which still chooses light or dark. `site.css` no longer contains any data URIs.
  - This follows the "many small modules" principle, and nobody hand-edits 1 KB URL strings again.
- **HIG §4** gains the rule: linen never repeats visibly; don't hand-edit it; repeated cards take a seeded offset.

## Verification

- **Builder equivalence:** with the old parameters (280 px, full strength), the builder's output matched the old hand-written tiles character for character, apart from number formatting (`0.1000` vs `0.1`). This was checked with a temporary test that wasn't committed.
- **Tests (`views/linen`):**
  - Near and Far are coprime;
  - each cloth is defined once, as a Near tile then a Far tile;
  - all four noises are stitched;
  - no seed is repeated within a cloth;
  - offsets are stable per key and stay within their tile, with at least 195 distinct among 200 cards.
- **Chrome:** journal cards had different `background-position` pairs, and the page and cards each had two linen layers. The owner reviewed it locally and said "lgtm ship it".
