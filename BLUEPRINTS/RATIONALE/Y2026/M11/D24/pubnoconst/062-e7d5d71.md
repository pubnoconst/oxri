## Context

The owner: "Lets make the text of embedded cards thinner and smaller. And also can we remove any bold letters? Bold is not the answer, bigger text is."

The audit found:
- **Explicit weights:** almost everything was already 400. The exception was the clipping author's name, at 500.
- **Default bold:** `<strong>` used the browser's default weight. That covers Make Room's figures and counts, and `**…**` in entries and books (for example "Causal Succession" in Book I).
- **Loaded faces:** Google Fonts served weights 400–700 that nothing needed. No italic face was loaded, so italics were synthesised obliques.

## Decision

- **Everything is weight 400:**
  - the font request is `ital,wght@0,400;1,400`, so only regular and its true italic load (fewer bytes, and real italics);
  - `html { font-synthesis-weight: none }` stops the browser faking bold;
  - `h1–h6`, `th`, `b`, `strong` and `summary` are explicitly 400.
- **Emphasis without weight:**
  - `strong` and `b` are set in small capitals with 0.03em tracking, the book way to stress a phrase. `**Causal Succession**` reads in small caps.
  - Where the thing to stress is a figure, which small caps wouldn't change, it's **larger**: Make Room's space-to-free figure is 1.2× its sentence.
  - Lighter emphasis stays italic.
- **Clippings (`views/tweet`) read as quoted matter:**
  - text drops from 1rem to 0.93rem, and the fine print scales with it: head 0.7 → 0.66, quoted text 0.92 → 0.86, byline 0.84 → 0.8, quoted byline and link 0.82 → 0.78;
  - the ink is mixed 84% toward the slip;
  - the author's name drops weight 500 and is set apart by full ink beside the dimmed handle.
  - EB Garamond has no weight below 400, so "thinner" is delivered by size and ink, never by opacity or another face.
- **HIG §2:**
  - "No bold. Bold is not the answer; bigger text is.";
  - stress by size or full ink;
  - small caps for strong emphasis;
  - quoted matter is smaller and softer.

  The old "500 for emphasis" line is gone.
- **`TestNoBold`** (routes):
  - rejects any `font-weight` other than 400, `normal` or `inherit`, and bold in a `font` shorthand;
  - requires `font-synthesis-weight: none`;
  - checks that the font request loads only 400 and its italic.

## Verification

- **Chrome, on an in-memory test server:**
  - an entry's `strong` computes to weight 400 with `small-caps`, and `em` is italic;
  - the only Garamond faces loaded are `400 normal` and `400 italic`;
  - "Causal Succession" renders in small capitals, and "Book I" in a true italic;
  - clipping text computes to 14.88 px at weight 400 against the entry's 15.68 px, and the author's name is 400.
- vet and `CGO_ENABLED=0 go test -count=1 ./...` pass.
