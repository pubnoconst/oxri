## Context

The owner: "The site is not very ambitious… It's because I play it safe. That said we still can make the UI/UX more consistent… how buttons/links behave inconsistently: ie scale up/light up etc. No HIG to discern between a link and a button."

An audit of every `:hover`, `:focus` and `:active` rule found six hover behaviours:
- scale up 4%: the landing menu, Log in / Log out / Settings;
- slide left: Back;
- lift 2 px with an ad-hoc shadow: catalog cards;
- dim to full: Sign out, Clear, the entry date, "View on X", "Return home", the sign-in disclosure;
- an underline: More tags;
- tint or border changes, with three different tint strengths: buttons and pills.

The encouraged button went translucent (opacity 0.88). Focus was a mix: some rules shared hover, some controls had nothing, and buttons used the browser default.

Link styles also varied:
- underlined at rest: entries, "Return home", Clear;
- never underlined: elsewhere;
- dim levels of 0.55, 0.6, 0.62, 0.65, 0.72 and 0.8.

## Decision

**A link goes somewhere; a button does something.** Each kind has one look and one response, defined once in `site.css` §3b and applied by class:

| Kind | At rest | Hover / focus | Pressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prose link (inside entries, books, link fallback cards) | underlined | underline thickens to 2 px | — |
| `.link-plain` (landing menu, Return home) | full ink | underline appears | — |
| `.link-quiet` (Back, Author sign in / Sign out, Log in / Log out / Settings, Clear, entry date, View on X, More tags, "Or sign in with…") | `--quiet-opacity` 0.65 | full ink, underline appears | — |
| `.btn` | depth-1 control | neutral tints 6% ink and its edge darkens; destructive tints 10% danger; encouraged deepens its fill (no translucency) | sinks 1 px |
| Pills | small control | tint 6% ink (both pill kinds; were 7% and 8%), edge darkens | — |
| Whole card | card | edge darkens (no lift, no ad-hoc shadow) | — |

- **Nothing moves or grows on hover or focus.** Removed:
  - the landing scale;
  - the back-link slide;
  - the card lift;
  - the play-mark scale.

  The only movement left is a pressed button sinking 1 px (`.btn:active:not(:disabled)`).
- **One focus ring:** `:where(a, button, summary, input[type=checkbox], [tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"])):focus-visible` gets a 2 px `--border-focus` outline, 2 px out. `:where()` keeps it at zero specificity, so a component may place it differently but never lose it. Text fields keep their focus border.
- **One timing:** `--interact-duration`, 140 ms, colour and opacity only.
- **Exceptions are written down:**
  - Sign out and Log out are `<button>`s wearing `.link-quiet`, because they POST but sit with links.
  - "Add new entry", Edit and pagination are links wearing `.btn`, because they sit among buttons.

  The element is chosen by what it does; the class by where it sits.
- **Per-page hover rules were deleted** from journals, landing, login, error page and tweet CSS, along with the duplicate prose-link rules in the editor and journals stylesheets.
- **`TestHoverAndFocusNeverMove`** (routes) parses every stylesheet and fails if a hover or focus rule scales, translates or rotates.
- **HIG:**
  - §5 gains "Links and buttons: which is which" (table, rules, exceptions);
  - §5 Buttons states the hover per kind;
  - §7 timing is 140 ms;
  - §9 has one focus ring;
  - §3's stale `--surface-subtle` is now `--depth-sunk-surface` (left over from RATIONALE 059).
