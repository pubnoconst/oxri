## Problem

On the owner's Pixel 10, the ripple shown while journals load tweets was barely noticeable. The owner then confirmed that tapping Book I or any link made **no ripple at all**.

**Cause: the quality governor (RATIONALE 039) had switched the water off, permanently.**
- Touch phones start at level 2, and every press ripple slower than about 42 fps (p75 > 24 ms) stepped down one level at once. Two choppy taps were enough to go 2 → 1 → 0.
- Level 0 was a trap:
  - nothing runs at 0, so nothing is measured and the level can never be earned back;
  - the level was stored in `localStorage` with no expiry.
- At 0, `attach()` is a no-op. The loading ripple loses its refraction and keeps only its painted trough (3.5% in light mode, 10% in dark), which by design (RATIONALE 038, "water shows as refraction, not paint") is nearly invisible on its own.

## Decision

- **Off is not forever.** Setting level 0 records the time in `res-imperii.water-off-at`. On load, a stored 0 older than 3 days starts at level 1, and so does an undated 0 (stored before this rule, as on the Pixel now).
- **Off takes more proof than other steps.** 1 → 0 needs two consecutive slow runs; 3 → 2 → 1 still step down at once.
- **Measure the press ripple alone.** A run during which other water was active (a card or page-wide loading ripple) is discarded. Those coincide with tweet iframes and HTMX swaps, and the governor can't tell whose frames were slow.
- **When refraction is off, paint a quiet crest.** `:root.water-off .card-water-ripple .card-water-wave` swaps in a slightly stronger trough and a soft highlight, from theme tokens set for each theme block. It is still subtle, but waiting is visible. With refraction on, nothing changes: paint stays near zero and the weave does the work.

## Not done (noted in NEXT.md)

- **If the Pixel is genuinely too slow at level 1,** it will turn off again after two slow taps, and loading ripples will fall back to the painted crest. The lasting fix is cheaper refraction for phones: limit the filter to the wave's bounding box, or a ring-only transform/opacity tier.
- **Sizing the loading ripple's duration and reach to phone-sized cards** waits on how it feels after this ships.

## Verification (Chrome, throwaway in-memory server with three tweet entries)

- **Startup:** an undated 0 starts at 1 (no `.water-off`); a 4-day-old 0 starts at 1; a 1-hour-old 0 stays 0 with `.water-off`.
- **Stepping:** at level 1, one slow run holds and two go to 0 (the time is recorded); slow, smooth, slow holds at 1; level 2 still drops to 1 on one slow run.
- **Painted crest:** loading rings were frozen mid-flight at level 0. The crest is visible but quiet in dark and light mode (the computed background uses the new tokens).
- **Level 2:** unchanged. The wave background is the old faint trough, and the cloth carries the refraction filter.
