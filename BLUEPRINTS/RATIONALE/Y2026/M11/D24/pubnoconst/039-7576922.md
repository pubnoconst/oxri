## Regression

Reported by the owner after #36 / #37: **clicking a journal entry crashed the client.**

Reproduced in Chrome with a genuine mouse click on a `/journals` entry: the renderer froze and never recovered. Isolation:
- **With the press ripple off** (governor level 0), the same click navigated normally.
- **The ripple alone, no navigation**, window visible: **1 frame in ~1.4 s** (about 1,025 ms per frame).

**Cause:** the press ripple applied its SVG displacement filter to the **root element**, so the entire document went through the filter, including `/journals`' seven cross-origin tweet iframes. Chrome must composite every out-of-process iframe into the filter's input on each frame, which is catastrophically slow. A navigation started on top of that stall (with a cross-document view transition snapshotting the outgoing page) hung the tab. The landing page has no iframes, which is why it measured a clean 60 fps during development.

## Why it shipped (honest account)

- **Development tests only dispatched `pointerdown`**, so a real navigation never ran during a ripple.
- **Covered-window throttling was over-applied as an explanation.** During #36 development, Chrome stopped drawing the automation window at times (about 1 frame per second even with no filter), and stalls measured on `/journals` were attributed to that. Some were this real stall.
- **The governor ignored samples at or above 250 ms as "covered window"**, so it was blind to exactly this pathology. A guard against a measuring artefact hid the real problem.

## Fix

- **The water lives under the cloth.** Page-scale ripples (the press ripple, and the `/journals` loading ripple) now refract `.water-cloth`: a fixed, viewport-sized layer of page linen behind all content, created by `ResImperiiWater.cloth()`. Cards, text and iframes are never inside the filter, so the cost is the same small amount on every page. This also fits the design: cards are cloth panels standing above the page's cloth (RATIONALE 030); the water moves beneath them.
  - Consequence: the page linen is now fixed while content scrolls over it. It's imperceptible at the weave's subtlety, and reads as cloth beneath.
  - Card-level ripples (a card waiting for its tweet) still refract their own card, as before. The tweet stays hidden until the ripple has faded.
- **The ripple stops the moment a navigation commits** (`pageswap`, plus `pagehide` as a fallback), so the outgoing page is snapshotted and torn down with no filter running.
- **The governor counts stalls as evidence.** The 250 ms "throttled, ignore" rule is gone; hidden tabs are still excluded.
- **Engine simplification:** the root-only `viewportRegion` mode is removed (the cloth is viewport-sized). Off-screen layer culling now applies to every stage. `.water-stage` no longer paints its own linen (it would hide the cloth), and its now-unused `data-water-stage` hook is removed.

## Verification

- `/journals` at level 3 with 7 iframes, window visible: **1 frame in 1.4 s → 83 frames, median 16.6 ms, p95 18.2 ms**. The filter is on the cloth; the root has none.
- The exact repro, genuine mouse clicks on two different journal entries (one with a tweet): both navigate and render, with no hang.
- gofmt, vet and `CGO_ENABLED=0 go test -count=1 ./...` pass.

## Lessons

- Test the real interaction (pointer *and* click *and* navigation), not a dispatched fragment of it.
- Before attributing a measurement to the environment, confirm the environment (a screenshot proves the window is drawing).
- Never let a noise filter discard the very signal it exists to catch.
