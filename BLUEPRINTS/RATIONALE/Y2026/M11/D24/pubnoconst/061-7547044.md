## Context

The owner: "The raw media card and the embedded clip card do not share the same border radius. Please fix and document in the HIG."

- A raw image, video or iframe laid on a card used `--radius-card` (0.5rem, 8 px).
- The post clipping (RATIONALE 057) used a literal 3 px, and the images inside it 2 px.
- Side by side in the catalog, they read as different kinds of object, though both sit at depth 2 (RATIONALE 059).

## Decision

- **Everything laid on a card uses `--radius-card`,** the same as the card: post clippings, raw images, video, iframes, link cards.
- **Anything nested inside those steps down one,** to `--radius-control`: a clipping's images (already true of its quoted post).
- **HIG §4 Radii** records both rules, and that surfaces take radii only from the tokens (or `50%` for a circle). Two named exceptions:
  - X's own embed (14 px), which matches the frame X draws inside its iframe;
  - the checkbox's drawn parts, which are a glyph, not a surface.
- **`TestRadiiComeFromTokens`** (routes) parses every stylesheet and rejects any other literal `border-radius`. An exception must be named for every selector in its rule.

## Verification

- **Chrome, on an in-memory test server:** the raw image and the clipping both compute to 8 px, and the image inside a clipping to 5.6 px.
- **The guard test** passes, and against the previous CSS it reports exactly the clipping's 3 px and its images' 2 px.
- `CGO_ENABLED=0 go test -count=1 ./...` passes.
- **Owner:** "ship it".
