## Context

The owner: "Linen was introduced because we wanted to make the water ripple look realistic without the need of an artificial shadow. We can remove it by defining css variables by depth and documenting these variables in the HIG instead."

The ripple was retired in RATIONALE 047. Linen had since become its own system:
- four SVG-noise cloths generated in Go (`views/linen`, RATIONALE 054);
- a separate stylesheet and route;
- a per-card weave offset.

It was doing a job (telling surfaces apart) that plain tokens can do.

## Decision

- **Every surface sits at one depth.** Each depth is a surface colour with the shadow that goes with it, defined for both themes in `site.css`:

  | Depth | Tokens | For |
  |---|---|---|
  | sunk | `--depth-sunk-surface` | wells: inputs, code, quoted posts |
  | 0 | `--depth-0-surface` | the page |
  | 1 | `--depth-1-surface`, `--depth-1-shadow` | cards, panels, buttons |
  | 2 | `--depth-2-surface`, `--depth-2-shadow` | laid on a card: clippings, embedded media |

- **Light comes from above:**
  - In light, depth 1 is `--page` mixed 55% toward white (the old card colour) and depth 2 is 80%.
  - In dark, depth 1 rises 4.5% toward white and depth 2 rises 8%. Depth 1 was 1.5% when the weave did the separating; without it, 1.5% barely separates a card from the page.
- **Shadows carry the height:**
  - depth 1 keeps the old card shadow (long and soft);
  - depth 2 takes the clipping's short, close shadow, now a token, with a stronger dark variant.
  - Embedded images, video, iframes and audio move from the card shadow to depth 2, since they're laid on the card.
- **One naming system, no aliases:**
  - `--fabric-page` becomes `--depth-0-surface`;
  - `--fabric-card` and `--surface-card` become `--depth-1-surface`;
  - `--shadow-card` becomes `--depth-1-shadow`;
  - `--surface-subtle` becomes `--depth-sunk-surface`;
  - the clipping's hard-coded `--page` background and shadow become the depth-2 pair.
- **Removed:**
  - `views/linen`, its tests, and its route and stylesheet link;
  - `linen.Offset` on journal cards;
  - the four `--linen-*` tokens and their theme switches.

  That's about 280 lines.
- **Documentation:**
  - HIG §4 gains the depth table and its rules: use the pair together, never an in-between depth, borders are for edges and not height, plus a note on why linen existed.
  - Principle 2 becomes "Depth, not decoration".
  - The README and `NEXT.md` design notes are updated.
