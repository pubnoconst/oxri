## Problem (owner)

"When the journals catalog first loads, there is an ugly off-centre loading message on the top, probably it's waiting on Turso. This message should be right where the bottom-centre loading messages are displayed when someone clicks on Journals from the landing page."

**The two loaders were in different places:**
- The `/journals` shell renders instantly and then fetches `/journals/content` (Turso) over HTMX. While it waited, its placeholder ("Loading entries…") sat in normal flow at the top-left of the catalog area.
- The loader that brought the reader there, the slow-navigation matrix from clicking Journals on the landing page (RATIONALE 046/047), sits fixed at the bottom centre.
- So one wait jumped from the bottom of the screen to the top.

## Decision

- **One place for waiting.** A new `.page-dot-loading` shares `.nav-dot-loading`'s fixed, bottom-centre position, which clears the safe-area inset.
  - The catalog shell's placeholder uses it, with `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`.
  - It sits inside `#journals-catalog-stage`, so the HTMX swap still replaces it with the catalog.
  - Arriving from the landing page now reads as one continuous wait: "Loading" becomes "Loading entries…" in the same spot.
- **No breathing text:** the placeholder's own opacity "breathe" animation is removed. The twinkle is the motion, per the HIG.
- **`HIG.md` §6:** a page waiting on its own content shows its loader in the same bottom-centre spot, never at the top.

## Verification (Chrome, local server)

- The landing page's slow-navigation loader and the catalog shell's real placeholder (fetched from `/journals` and put back into the stage) were measured in the same 1,037 px window. Both are centred at x = 519 and sit 20 px from the bottom.
- The placeholder's matrix is a running twinkle, and its text is "Loading entries…".
