- **Committed**: 2026-11-24 13:27:20 +1000
- **Committer**: `pubnoconst`

- **Merged**: PR #41

## Decision (owner)

Journal entries are short-form: cap the body at **2,000 characters**. Previously a journal body shared the books' long-form limit of 100,000 characters.

## How

- **A journal-specific limit:** `domain.MaxJournalBodyLength = 2000`, enforced in `ParseJournalEntry` (the single validation boundary since RATIONALE 014). The error reads "journal content is N characters; the maximum is 2000".
  - The shared `DefaultMaxProseLength` (100,000) and `db.SanitizeProseInput`, which also serve Books I–III, are untouched. Books stay long-form.
- **Characters, not bytes:** the count is in Unicode code points, as all existing prose validation does. Accented Latin (`ō`) and Greek (`λ`) take 2 bytes each in UTF-8 but count as one character. The tests pin this: 2,000 Greek characters (about 4,000 bytes) are accepted.
- **One source of truth for the number:**
  - The editor's textarea gets `maxlength` from the Go constant, and so do its `n / 2000` counter and its "Up to 2,000 characters." hint.
  - `journal-editor.js` reads the limit from `maxlength` rather than hard-coding it. (The older media-link and tag counters still duplicate their limits in JS; folding them in belongs to plan step 3, typed form structs.)
  - Over the cap, the counter turns red, a plain message appears ("Too long: 2100 characters. The limit is 2000."), and Publish is disabled.
  - The server refuses over-cap bodies regardless; `maxlength` only stops typing and pasting.
- **Existing data was checked before changing the rule:** a throwaway, read-only tool (deleted afterwards; it printed lengths only, never content) found all 7 entries under the cap, the longest at 326 characters. No entry becomes uneditable.

## Verification

- **Domain:** exactly-at and one-over the cap for ASCII, Greek and accented Latin.
- **End to end:** posting 2,001 characters stores nothing; 2,000 Greek characters are accepted. The new-entry editor renders `maxlength="2000"`, the counter and the hint. (The form is pre-filled with 167 characters of starter text, so the counter starts at `167 / 2000`, not 0.)
- **Chrome:** typing " Λόγος" moved the counter 167 → 173 (each Greek letter counts as one). A 2,100-character body showed the red counter and the message, and disabled Publish.
