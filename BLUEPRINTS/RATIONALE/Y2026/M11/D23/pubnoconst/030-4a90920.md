## Why texture

On a flat colour, refraction is invisible: bending a uniform field changes nothing. So the water ripple had to be *painted*, with a white sunlit crest, a dark trough and a centre drop. On a woven surface, the existing `water-ripple.js` displacement visibly bends the threads, which is how real water reads. The texture lets the ripple be natural, and lets the painted parts go.

The inspiration was the grey linen of Mac OS X Lion (10.7: login window, Mission Control, Notification Center). This is an **original** CSS weave, not a reproduction of Apple's asset.

## How the linen is drawn

- An inline-SVG tile (`data:` URI in a CSS custom property, about 0.9 KB each, no image files; CSP `img-src data:` was already allowed).
- Two `feTurbulence` fractal-noise fields: one fine across and long along x (horizontal threads), one transposed (vertical threads). Averaged, they give linen's slubby, irregular weave instead of a mechanical grid.
- One `feColorMatrix` maps the noise above the mean to faint **dark** fibres and another maps the noise below the mean to faint **light** fibres. The same tile therefore reads over any base colour; each theme only tunes the two strengths.
- Tiles were generated from one parameterised script and tuned by eye in Chrome against journal text in both themes.

## Composition

- **Page = one cloth:** `--fabric-page` on `body` and on every surface the ripple's filter is applied to (`.water-stage`, `.auth-water-stage`, `.loading-page`, `.loading-overlay`). A filter can only bend texture inside the filtered element, so each stage carries its own copy, aligned with the body.
- **Each card = its own cloth:** `--fabric-card` (a separately seeded tile) on journal cards, the filter and batch bars, vacate, auth and settings cards, editor panes, the book preview and the X fallback card, lifted by a layered `--shadow-card`. Inputs, buttons and the Markdown textarea stay smooth, so fields still read as fields.
- **Light:** ivory cards (`page 45% + white`) on flax, with a soft warm shadow. Approved by the author as-is.
- **Dark:** the first pass was too strong, and a lighter card tone made cards look like a translucent overlay. The fix: page fibres about 35% weaker, cards on almost the same base (`page 98.5% + white`), and cards **told apart by their weave**: the page is a fine linen with long threads, the card a coarser cross-weave with shorter slubs (`baseFrequency 0.6/0.11` vs `0.9/0.055`). This was verified side by side at 3×.

## Ripple

- `--ripple-sun` is `transparent` in every theme. A hard-coded `rgba(255,255,255,0.14)` stop in the wave gradient was removed too. The `.card-water-drop` centre dot was removed from CSS and from every markup and JS source (`journal-media.js`, `journals.go`, `login.go`, `loading.go`).
- Only a faint `--ripple-trough` band remains (light `0.035`, dark `0.1`). The waves still animate opacity and scale, which is what drives the refraction strength.
- `/loading` and the submit overlay now refract the whole linen surface rather than only the phrase. Amplitude went from 6 to 9 because, with the crest gone, the bent weave is the main cue.

Guiding principle from review: **subtlety is the key**.
