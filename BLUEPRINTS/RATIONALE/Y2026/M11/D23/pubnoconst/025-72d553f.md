### Summary
- **Per-IP Lockout & Bounded Auth State** (`internal/auth`): Isolated failed TOTP attempt lockouts and 30s single-flight cooldowns per client IP so an attacker IP cannot lock out the administrator's IP; bounded pending TOTP/WebAuthn maps, active sessions, and failure tracking maps.
- **Persistent TOTP Replay Watermark (Schema v6)** (`internal/db/auth_state.go`, `internal/auth/totp.go`): Monotonically persists `last_totp_step` in Turso `auth_state` across process restarts.
- **Hardened Reverse Proxy IP Trust** (`routes/middleware.go`): Honors forwarded headers (`True-Client-IP`, `CF-Connecting-IP`, rightmost untrusted `X-Forwarded-For` hop) and `X-Forwarded-Proto: https` only when `TRUST_PROXY=true` and `r.RemoteAddr` is a trusted loopback/private/CGNAT (`100.64.0.0/10`) peer; bounded `ipRateLimiter` growth.
- **POST-Only `/logout`** (`routes/routes.go`, landing/journals views): Changed `/logout` to `POST`-only (`GET` returns `405`) and updated navigation links to inline `<form method="POST" action="/logout">` buttons styled invisibly as quiet text links.
- **Fresh Step-Up Reauthentication for Passkey Management** (`serverfns/auth.go`, `routes/passkeys.go`, `views/pilot/settings.go`, `views/login/login.go`): Gated `/pilot/passkeys/begin`, `finish`, and `delete` behind a 5-minute `RequireFreshAuth` window with `?reauth=1` step-up prompt.
- **Strict `script-src` CSP** (`routes/middleware.go`, `assets/js/navigation-feedback.js`): Removed `'unsafe-inline'` from `script-src` by externalizing form confirm prompts via `data-confirm`.
- **Offline Turso Snapshot CLI** (`internal/db/backup.go`, `cmd/backup/main.go`, `mage backup`): Exports journals, books, and passkeys to timestamped `0600` JSON backups in `../oxri-parent/backups/` with transactional `-restore` support.
- **Documentation Sync**: Updated `README.md` and `BLUEPRINTS/NEXT.md` to remove stale loopback SSH and `rawcontent/` references.
