## Context

The whole site was set in EB Garamond, one family by rule (HIG §2). The owner felt the serif is right for the books but not necessarily for the site's UI and the journals. They proposed:
- Figtree or Lato for the UI.
- Playpen Sans for entries.
- Playwrite USA for quotes, "to denote difference in authorship".

They flagged Greek as a likely problem.

## What changed

| Role | Face | Token |
|---|---|---|
| The books: book pages, the landing's title and book links | EB Garamond | `--font-serif` |
| Site UI: journals, login, settings, editor, error pages, buttons | Figtree | `--font-ui` |
| An entry's own words | Playpen Sans | `--font-entry` |
| Quoted matter: tweet clippings, Bible passages, blockquotes in entries | Happy Monkey | `--font-quote` |

- Each of the three new stacks falls back to Didact Gothic for Greek.
- All faces are loaded at weight 400 only.
- HIG §2 is rewritten: "the face says who wrote it".

## Decisions

- **Figtree over Lato**: its Latin file is about half the size (11 KB vs 24 KB). Neither has Greek.
- **Playwrite dropped**: it is a cursive handwriting face, and a 1,000-character passage at clipping size would be hard to read on a budget phone. The owner chose Happy Monkey instead after seeing it.
- **Greek coverage**, checked against Google Fonts' own stylesheets:
  - Figtree, Lato, Happy Monkey and Playwrite have none.
  - Playpen Sans has monotonic Greek but no polytonic.
  - Didact Gothic has full polytonic and plain, rounded shapes close to all three new faces.
  - The browser falls back letter by letter, so naming Didact Gothic after each face covers every Greek letter the prose whitelist allows.

## Verification

- Browser check on the local server: every face loads. The login page renders in Figtree. A KJV verse plus ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος set in each stack: Happy Monkey joins Didact Gothic's Greek well.
- `go vet`, `staticcheck` and all tests pass.

## Not done

- **Mixed Greek in entries**: in a Playpen entry, plain Greek (λόγος) comes from Playpen and accented Greek (ἀρχῇ) from Didact Gothic, so one phrase changes face midway. Routing all Greek to Didact Gothic needs self-hosted fonts with `unicode-range`. That would also remove the third-party font request on budget phones.
- The offline page keeps its inline EB Garamond.
- Book editor headers now use the UI face. Book reading pages are unchanged.
