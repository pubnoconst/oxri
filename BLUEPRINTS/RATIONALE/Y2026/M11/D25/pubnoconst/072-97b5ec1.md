## Context

After 071 the owner said the dropdown background "appears too cool for the rest of the site", and asked whether they were seeing things. They were not. Then they found the open list "floating disconnected" and asked for a connected dropdown, "the border should surround the text box as well".

## What changed

- **Warm glass.**
  - The float glass was lifted 12% toward pure white, and its backdrop saturated 1.8×. Saturation amplifies the backdrop's own cast, and the dark page is slightly blue.
  - Measured as red minus blue of the glass composited over a card, the glass came to about −5 against the card's −4.
  - It now lifts 14% toward warm paper (`--glass-light`, `#f5f0e6`), the same for the edge and the top highlight. Both glasses use one `--glass-saturate` of 1.2 (it was 1.8 for the float and 1.4 for the veil).
  - Result: dark −3 against the card's −4; light +3 against the card's +2.
- **Connected lists.** Select lists (via `:open` and `::picker(select)`) and the Book list (via `.book-search:has(.book-list:not([hidden]))`) now hang flush from their field:
  - One outline in `--border-focus` runs around field and list.
  - The field loses its lower corners and its focus ring.
  - The list loses its upper corners, has a `--border-subtle` divider and no gap.
  - Lists open downward only (`position-try-fallbacks: none`), as tall as the room below (`max-height: 100%` of the position area), scrolling for more. A flip above the field would detach the list.
- **HIG §4**:
  - Glass is warm like the paper, never lifted toward pure white.
  - Warmth is checked by number: the glass must be no cooler than the card.
  - A list that opens from a field is connected to it.
