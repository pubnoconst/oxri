## Context

The owner reported: "when I search something in journals catalog the results contain cards that do not center preview media at the center." A search usually returns few entries, so one card can fill the whole row, and the media rules had only been tested at two-column width.

## What changed

- **Clipping photos** (`views/tweet/tweet.css`): a single attachment carries an inline `aspect-ratio`, and the catalog caps `.tweet-media` at `13.5rem` high. On a wide card the cap narrows the block through its aspect ratio (Chrome measured 295 px inside an 820 px box), and the block stayed at the left edge. `margin: 0.7rem auto 0` centres it. When the block fills the width, the auto margins are zero and nothing changes.
- **Linked images** (`views/journals/journals.css`): `width: 100%; object-fit: cover` cropped a wide card's image to a thin strip, cutting off heads. The owner chose "show more". The image now keeps its own shape under the same `13.5rem` cap and is centred by the slot (`site.css .journal-preview-media`, `display: flex; justify-content: center`). Video keeps its cover rule.

## Verification

- `/journals?q=prince` (one linked image): the whole statue shows, centred.
- `/journals`, two columns: both kinds stay whole and centred.
