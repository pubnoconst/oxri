## Context

The owner: "Clicking on the empty space after the preview text in the catalog won't land you to that entry. Only clicking on the top end does."

## Cause

A catalog card is made clickable by a stretched link: the date link's `::after` covers the card (`position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 1`, the card being `position: relative`). The date was dimmed with `opacity`, by `.link-quiet` and by `.entry-date` (0.65).

- **The overlay was sealed in.** Opacity below 1 makes an element its own stacking context, so the overlay's `z-index` counted only inside the date link.
- **The excerpt covered it.** The excerpt is dimmed too (0.88) and stretches to fill the card's spare height (`flex: 1`). It is painted as a layer later in the document, so wherever its box reached, including the blank space below its last line, `elementFromPoint` returned the excerpt, not the link.
- **Only the top worked:** at the card's top nothing dimmed overlapped the overlay.

## What changed

- **The date link no longer uses `link-quiet`;** `.entry-date` is quiet by colour (`color-mix` at 65 % of the ink), with the same underline on hover.
- **The overlay covers the whole card again.** The card's controls stay above it: they are raised to `z-index: 2`.
- **`TestCardLinkCoversTheWholeCard`** fails if the date link or `.entry-card-link` is dimmed by `opacity`. Putting the opacity back made it fail.

## Verification

In Chrome against real data, on every card:
- the blank space below the text, the text and the media area hit the card link;
- Edit, Delete, the selection checkbox and "View on X" each receive their own clicks.
