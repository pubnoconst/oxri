## Context

The owner asked: "How come 4chan can display 150 posts in their catalog and we struggle with 15?" They then asked for larger catalogs ("like 25"), and for no GIF or media to autoplay unless tapped. Then: "I still want thumbnails of videos or gifs, not autoplay/prefetch", with the cards' contents centred.

During UI testing they also asked for:
- the select box on the left, the date and bookmark on the right;
- a Bible passage's reference centred;
- the preview keeping its line breaks, with more than two in a row ignored;
- the same card width everywhere, with a lone card centred.

## Measured first

The lab gained `-seed N` (entries, each tweet clipping with a 1200×900 photo) and `-page-size`. X's image requests are served by the lab over the throttled network, one URL per image: a shared URL let the cache serve 11 of 12 images. The lab also counts image bytes, since the browser reports cross-origin sizes as 0.

With 160 seeded entries, going from 12 to 150 cards per page moved LCP from 1.10 s to 1.26 s. There was no shift and no long frames while scrolling. The old cap of 12 dated from X's iframes (045); the cards are now server-drawn HTML with lazy images.

## What changed

- **24 per page.** It fills 1, 2, 3 or 4 columns evenly, unlike 25.
- **Equal card widths.**
  - The grid is a size container. Each card is `(100cqw − gaps) / columns`, where the column count comes from container queries at 18.5 rem per column.
  - A short last row, or a lone search result, keeps that width and is centred.
- **Card layout.**
  - Contents are centred; a quoted clipping keeps its own left-aligned text.
  - The select box is on the left, the date and badges on the right.
  - The Bible reference is centred.
  - The excerpt keeps line breaks (`white-space: pre-line`):
    - each block ends with a blank line, `<br>` gives a newline;
    - three or more newlines collapse to two;
    - spaces within a line collapse.
- **X image sizes.** A card asks for `?name=small` (680 px), an entry page for `medium`. That's 68 kB instead of 206 kB per image: 2.5 MB → 0.8 MB on a 24-card page.
- **Server stills** (`internal/thumb`, table `media_thumbs`, schema v10).
  - **What gets a still:**
    - a linked image;
    - a GIF (its first frame);
    - a YouTube video (`hqdefault`);
    - a Vimeo video (via oEmbed).
  - **How it's made:** fetched once in the background (`thumb.Filler`, like tweet snapshots), or within 5 s on save. It's kept as a JPEG at most 680 px wide and served at `/thumb/{key}.jpg?v=…`, cached for good.
  - **What fetches are allowed:**
    - only public addresses, checked by a dialer `Control` hook on the IP actually dialled, so DNS rebinding to a private address is refused too;
    - at most 3 redirects, a 12 MB download, and 40 MP decoded.
  - **Failures:** an unusable link is recorded for a week. Stills of media no entry links to are pruned.
- **Nothing plays until tapped.**
  - A GIF or video card is a tap tile drawn by the server, with its still and the play mark. The tap builds the player in the same space.
  - Without a still, the script draws a tile with the media's name.
  - Audio uses `preload="none"`.
  - In a card, a linked image is the still itself; the entry page shows the original.

## Two regressions caught on real data

- **CLS 0.039 while scrolling.**
  - **Cause:** a lazily loaded still started as a 28 px placeholder and grew to 242 px. Its `width`/`height` attributes reserved nothing, because the shared rule `width: auto; height: auto` overrides them until the image arrives.
  - **Fix:** the still is sized from its known ratio, `min(100cqw, 100cqh × ratio)`, so it takes its final box before loading. CLS went back to 0.
  - The lab's shift report now names an image's source and parent, which is how this was found.
- **LCP 1.65 s.**
  - **Cause:** the video's still became the largest paint. When the script made it, the request waited 780 ms.
  - **Fix:** the server draws the tile and its still in the HTML, so the request starts at 51 ms. LCP fell to 1.26 s.
