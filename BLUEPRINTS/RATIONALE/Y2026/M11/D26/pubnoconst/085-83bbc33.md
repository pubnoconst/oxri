## Context

084 made the catalog preview keep the author's line breaks, with a paragraph break shown as one blank line. The owner: "in md, 2 lines mean one newline. So can you also ingore the secomd newlines in preview?"

## What changed

`Excerpt280` collapses every run of newlines to one (`\n{2,}` → `\n`, was `\n{3,}` → `\n\n`):
- a paragraph, heading or list item ends its line;
- a `<br>` ends its line;
- the preview never shows an empty line.

Spaces within a line still collapse.

## Verification

`TestExcerpt280`:
- `# Title` followed by a paragraph gives `"Title\nShort paragraph…"`;
- soft breaks, a run of blank lines, a `<br>` and a list give one line each, with no empty lines.
