## What shipped

### 1. Universal post and passage clippings (`views/tweet`, `views/passage`, `scripture-picker.js`, `journal-media.js`)

- **Top-left anchor:** every post clipping places its circular avatar, stacked `Name` and `@handle`, and quiet `↗` link at the top-left (dropping the duplicate post header date and bottom dashed-rule byline). Bible passages (`views/passage` and the editor's live preview in `scripture-picker.js`) share the same spine: reference at top-left, version at top-right, verses below.
- **Three post archetypes (`views/tweet.Classify`):**
  - **Caption (`1C`, `.is-caption`, `media` and `≤ 80` characters):** full-bleed media (`aspect-ratio` clamped between `0.85` and `1.85` so its slot height is known up front). Both the top-left frosted author pill (`--depth-overlay-blur`) and the bottom caption scrim start at `opacity: 0` and ease in (`240 ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, opacity only) on cursor hover, keyboard/tap focus (`:focus` / `:focus-within`), or central-band scroll focus on touch displays (`journal-media.js`).
  - **Newspaper snippet (`.is-snippet`, `media` and `> 80` characters):** same top-left author row and right-hand text as a pure statement, adding a compact `4.85rem` square thumbnail on the left (with video play mark or `1/N` photo count).
  - **Pure statement (`.is-statement`, no `media`):** top-left author row with full-width statement prose directly below.

### 2. Decluttered catalog preview cards (`views/journals`)

- Collapses each catalog card into three left-aligned layers that grow only when present: (1) clipping, passage, server still, or link slip, (2) entry excerpt (`--font-entry`), and (3) one quiet single-line footer (`Date · Tags` on the left, subtle `Draft` and `★` on the right), followed when signed in by the frozen author action bar (`TestCatalogCardsKeepTheirActions`).
- `.entry-date.entry-card-link` and its footer ancestors carry no `opacity` and no `position: relative`, keeping the stretched card link active across the entire card (`TestCardLinkCoversTheWholeCard`).

### 3. Newspaper full-entry reading layout (`/journals/{id}`, `views/journals.ClassifyEntryLayout`)

- **`wrap-left` (default):** inside a `display: flow-root` stage, the unclamped source floats top-left (`clamp(15.5rem, 43%, 22.5rem)`), and commentary starts at top-right on the same horizon, expanding to `100%` width once it clears the source (`< 680 px` viewports release the float to one column).
- **`stack-rich-source`:** when a source combines media with a `> 80`-character description (or media + quoted post), the source pairs a broadsheet left plate (`minmax(11rem, 38%)`) with unclamped right-hand text across the top, and commentary starts at the bottom.
- **`wrap-compact-code` vs. `stack-early-code`:** when `<pre><code>` appears before `< 420` characters of prose and the source is concise (`≤ 140` characters, no media), `.entry-compact-diptych` pairs the `~29%` source card and `~71%` opening prose + `<pre><code>` column with `align-items: stretch` so the source card's bottom aligns flush with the bottom of the code block and its contents sit vertically centred (`justify-content: center`), with remaining prose spanning `100%` below. Longer or media-bearing sources with early code fall back to `stack-early-code`.
