## Context

On `/journals/1795411807274` (a direct linked landscape image inside a `wrap-left` newspaper entry), `.entry-source-box` sat at the top-left, but `.journal-media-slot.is-image` reserved a fixed `26rem` (`416 px`) box while `.journal-preview-media` centred its child vertically (`align-items: center`), floating the `336 × 224 px` image `96 px` down from the top with `96 px` of empty space above and below.

## What changed

- **`views/journals` (`mediaSlot`):** when `item.Thumb` has an image still (`t.Kind == "image"`) on a full entry page (`!isCollage`), the slot now receives `has-known-ratio` and `style="--still-ratio: W / H; aspect-ratio: W / H"`, reserving the exact image shape up front without an oversized `26rem` box or any layout shift on load.
- **`views/journals/journals.css`:** `.journal-card.is-full .journal-preview-media` sets `align-items: flex-start; justify-content: flex-start;` so full-entry media always anchors flush at the top-left, and `.has-known-ratio` sizes cleanly under the `min(26rem, 80vw)` height cap.
